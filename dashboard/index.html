How to read this dashboard

All findings are associations from one survey taken at one point in time. They do not show cause. Percentages are unweighted, so they describe our sample, not the US population.

Target: 1 if heart attack or angina is "Yes", 0 if both are "No". 5,021 records with an unknown target are excluded. ChestScan and PneumoVaxEver are left out of the models because they may follow a diagnosis.

Read these with care

The difference is on the relative scale. In percentage points the gap in older adults is as large or larger: a stroke adds 22.7 points at ages 18–39 and 24.4 points at 65 and over. The survey cannot show which came first.
