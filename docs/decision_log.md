# Data Preparation Decision Log

## Decisions implemented

1. Preserve the original CSV unchanged.
   Reason: retain a reproducible source dataset.

2. Create a stable row_id from the original CSV row order.
   Reason: track records and share split assignments.
   The row_id is not a model predictor.

3. Define heart_disease as 1 when HadHeartAttack or HadAngina
   is Yes, and 0 only when both are No.
   Reason: follow the project target definition.

4. Exclude 5,021 records with unknown targets.
   Reason: their outcome cannot be determined.
   Do not impute target labels.

5. Exclude HadHeartAttack and HadAngina from predictors and Q2.
   Reason: both fields define the target.

6. Retain identical records initially.
   Reason: identical survey answers do not prove duplicate people.
   Keep each exact-record group within one split.
   Group identifiers are not model predictors.

7. Split record groups using stratification and seed 42.
   Assign approximately 70% training, 15% validation and 15% test.
   Frozen row assignments are in `data/manifests/split_manifest.csv`.
   Normal regeneration validates and reads this file; it never rewrites it.

8. Retain rows with missing predictor values.
   Reason: 135,348 training rows have at least one missing predictor.
   Complete-case deletion would discard about 43.9% of training rows.

9. Keep the handoff CSVs raw valued with missingness intact.
   `src/preprocessing.py` provides an unfitted training-median numeric
   transformer with missingness indicators and a categorical Missing
   category with one-hot encoding. Fit it inside each model CV fold.

10. Keep BMI and exclude height and weight from the primary model.
    Reason: reduce overlap between BMI and its component measurements.
    Retain original measurements for EDA and sensitivity analysis.

11. Do not automatically remove extreme numeric values.
    The implemented training range checks are review flags, including
    SleepHours outside the dictionary's 1-24 range.
    These checks do not establish that every extreme value is valid.

## Modelling decisions (Member 3, 14 Nov 2026)

12. Exclude `ChestScan` and `PneumoVaxEver` from the primary predictors.
    Reason: the timing of the scan or vaccination relative to the heart disease
    diagnosis is unknown. Both may be a result of the diagnosis: people with heart
    disease are scanned during cardiac work-up, and CDC recommends pneumococcal
    vaccination for adults with chronic heart disease. Using them risks reverse
    causality / temporal leakage. They stay in the data for EDA and Q2 context.
    Evidence (training split): 77.3% of heart-disease cases had a chest CT vs 39.0%
    of others; pneumococcal vaccination 67.0% vs 38.7%. The gaps hold within every
    age band and among never-smokers without COPD.
    Enforced in code: `src/modelling.py` (`POST_DIAGNOSIS_FEATURES`) and a check
    in `notebooks/04_modelling.ipynb` that stops if either column reaches the model.
    This decision was recorded before any model results were seen.

13. Model selection uses validation average precision (AP) only; the test split
    stays closed until all settings, preparation choices and thresholds are frozen
    in `results/modelling/frozen_settings.json`.

## Modelling robustness checks (Member 3)

14. Predictor ranking is stable. Permutation importance was repeated with seeds 7 and 21
    (random forest refitted per seed; same 10,000-row validation sample). Logistic
    regression gives the identical top five for seeds 42, 7 and 21: AgeCategory,
    GeneralHealth, Sex, HadStroke, HadKidneyDisease. The random forest keeps the same
    top four; its fifth place varies (HadCOPD / HadDiabetes / HadKidneyDisease, all about
    0.010 AP drop). Decision: these five go to Member 2 for Q2; the shared top four are
    the robust core and HadKidneyDisease is less certain.
    Evidence: results/modelling/ranking_stability.csv

15. Duplicate rows do not affect validation results: 21 duplicate groups with 22 extra
    copies (0.03%). One row per group leaves AP unchanged (LR 0.3606; RF 0.3580 -> 0.3581).
    Decision: the test evaluation uses all rows. Evidence: results/modelling/duplicate_check.csv

16. Gradient boosting added as a third model (validation only). Selected setting:
    learning_rate 0.05, max_leaf_nodes 31, min_samples_leaf 100 (tie rule), threshold 0.2055.
    Validation AP 0.364 vs 0.361 for logistic regression; F1 0.411 vs 0.409. Logistic
    regression stays the selected model: equal F1, higher precision (0.363 vs 0.337),
    fewer false alarms and interpretable odds ratios. Gradient boosting has the same top
    five predictors. Evidence: results/modelling/validation_metrics_three_models.csv

17. Odds ratios come from a separate unpenalised logistic regression on the training split
    with explicit reference categories (age 18-24, excellent health, female, "No" for
    conditions). Used for interpretation only, not for prediction. Its validation AP is
    0.3603. Evidence: results/modelling/odds_ratios.csv

18. Age-interaction stories: stroke, all teeth removed, kidney disease, sleeping 4 h or less
    and daily smoking each have a larger odds ratio at 18-39 than at 65+ (adjusted for sex,
    general health, diabetes and smoking), with interaction p < 1e-20 on training and the
    same ordering on validation. They are confirmed on the relative scale only; in
    percentage points the 65+ gap is the same or larger. Evidence:
    results/stories/age_interactions.csv

19. All of entries 12-18 were fixed before the test split was opened.

20. Q2 method (notebook 05): bias-corrected Cramér's V (categorical) or eta (numeric) per
    leading predictor x candidate, plus signed Spearman where both are ordered; observed pairs only.
    Candidates are the 34 model features (ChestScan, PneumoVaxEver, height and weight excluded), for consistency with Q1.
    The definitional pair Sex x HadDiabetes (pregnancy-only level) is kept but left out of top lists.
    Top 5 per predictor ranked on training, 95% CI from 200 bootstrap resamples by duplicate group.
    Confirmed on validation if |valid - train| <= 0.02 or valid is inside the training CI. Evidence: results/q2/q2_associations.csv
